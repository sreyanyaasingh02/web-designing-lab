Shoes are an important part of everyday life. From comfortable sneakers to formal footwear, every pair has its own style and purpose. Here are four popular types of shoes worth knowing about.

Our Shoe Collection

Sneakers

Sneakers are popular for casual wear. They are comfortable and suitable for everyday activities.

Price Range: ₹1,500 – ₹5,000

Formal Shoes

Formal shoes are commonly worn at offices, meetings and special occasions.

Price Range: ₹2,000 – ₹7,000

Sports Shoes

Sports shoes are designed for activities such as running, training and playing games.

Price Range: ₹1,800 – ₹6,000

Boots

Boots provide extra coverage and are often chosen for outdoor wear and different styles.

Price Range: ₹2,500 – ₹8,000
